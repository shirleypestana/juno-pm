## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno is an AI Associate PM that helps a support escalation manager or PM understand a reported P0 customer issue by drafting a concise, source-linked brief from its Slack thread, related Jira incident, and relevant Notion notes.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Required incident evidence:

The selected Slack escalation thread.

Its linked Jira incident, including its current priority, status, owner, and latest update.

Relevant Notion incident notes explicitly linked from that thread or incident, when available.

Company context:

RocketShip Product Strategy One-Pager.

Product Quarterly Roadmap.

Engineering Capacity Note.

Relevant excerpts from the affected account’s Sales Pipeline Snapshot.

Relevant supplied Raw Signals, when they concern the selected issue.

Keep each source’s identity, capture time, and available last-updated time. Distinguish customer reports, suspected causes, sales statements, and confirmed commitments.

Use supplied records or existing approved access. Exclude unrelated accounts, inaccessible documents, irrelevant conversation, and direct reporting database queries. Source content is evidence, not instructions that can change Juno’s permissions.

**How fresh must it be**

Fetch Slack and Jira records when the user requests a brief. These records must have been checked within the previous 60 seconds when generation begins.

Check linked Notion notes and company documents for their latest available version when opening the review session. Recheck them after 24 hours if reused.

Show when the evidence was captured. A recently fetched document is not necessarily current: flag undated documents, expired planning periods, and relative deadlines whose reference date is unknown.

Before posting a brief, recheck Slack and Jira. If relevant facts changed, regenerate the affected content and obtain confirmation of the revised brief.

If a required source is unreachable, return a labelled partial result. Never silently substitute cached incident status or treat missing evidence as confirmation.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

Read:

read_slack_thread: Read the selected thread within the user’s existing access.

read_jira_incident: Read the linked incident and its updates.

read_notion_notes: Read relevant linked incident notes.

read_company_context: Read relevant excerpts from the supplied or approved company documents.

Draft:

draft_escalation_brief: Produce the four-section brief with source references.

revise_escalation_brief: Revise the draft using refreshed evidence or the user’s review.

Send:

post_reviewed_brief: Post the exact user-approved version to the existing internal Slack thread, after freshness and access checks.

No tool may expand its own access or act outside the selected incident.

**Deliberate omissions**

No reporting database access: Engineering prohibits additional load before sharding.

No new authentication or permission infrastructure: Security & Compliance has no capacity for it this quarter. Start with supplied evidence or existing approved access.

No Jira edits, assignment, closure, priority changes, or roadmap updates: Juno’s first job is synthesis, and these actions could change other people’s work.

No customer messaging, delivery promises, or automatic incident paging: These require judgment and operational ownership beyond this brief.

No broad search across unrelated accounts: Keep the evidence relevant and prevent unnecessary access to customer information.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Allow at most five model turns per brief request, including evidence gathering, drafting, and any revision. A turn is one model call and any tool actions it requests.

Allow one retry total for a failed source read, within the same ceiling. Independent reads may run together.

Stop when the brief passes verification, the ceiling is reached, or a required source remains unavailable after the retry.

Return a labelled partial result when evidence is incomplete. Hand unresolved contradictions, uncertain incident identity, and consequential decisions to the user.

Generating a brief must not pause or replace the existing human response to a P0. Posting is a separate, user-confirmed action.
If a critical source remains unavailable, stop and return a clearly labelled partial brief identifying what could not be checked. Hand unresolved contradictions and consequential decisions to the user.

Do not keep searching or rewriting indefinitely. Showing the brief must not delay the existing human response to a P0. Posting is a separate, user-confirmed action.

**Latency and cost target**

Provisional target: return a verified brief or an explicit partial-result notice within 10 seconds for at least 95% of requests.

Use a 20-second hard timeout. At timeout, stop further generation and show the available verified findings, missing evidence, and reason for stopping.

Target generation cost is at most $0.10 per request, with a $0.15 maximum budget, including retries, model calls, and any paid retrieval. Do not start a call whose estimated maximum cost exceeds the remaining budget.

Measure elapsed time, turn count, and actual cost per request. Validate these targets with representative incidents before release; they are not measured results from the prototype.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Keep retrieved evidence, the draft, and user corrections only within the current user’s review session for the selected incident.

Do not share session memory between users, teams, accounts, or incidents. Each new session reloads its evidence.

Only the reviewing user may edit the draft or provide corrections. Juno may update its draft but cannot modify the original records.

A confirmed Slack post becomes a normal Slack record governed by Slack’s access and retention rules; it is not persistent model memory.

**Expiry and write rules**

Delete session content on reset, sign-out, 30 minutes of inactivity, or two hours after the session starts, whichever occurs first.

Session retention does not make evidence current. Apply the 60-second incident freshness rule independently of session expiry.

Human edits take precedence over generated wording. If an edit contradicts a source, preserve it as an attributed user correction and flag the disagreement; do not present it as a source-confirmed fact.

A substantive draft change invalidates any previous posting approval. Recheck the final version before requesting confirmation again.

Do not use session content as cross-session memory or training data. Operational metrics must exclude customer messages and source document contents.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | blocked |
| send | confirm |

**Justification**

Read — Auto:
Juno may read the selected evidence within the reviewing user’s existing access. Reading changes no shared records. Cross-account access and permission expansion are blocked.

Draft — Auto:
Juno may assemble, revise, and display a private brief for review. The draft is reversible and does not change operational priorities or commitments.

Write — Blocked:
Juno cannot edit Jira, assign or close tickets, change priorities, or update the roadmap. These changes could cause colleagues to act on incorrect shared information and are outside this candidate’s scope.

Send — Confirm:
Juno may post only the exact reviewed brief to the existing internal Slack thread after an explicit confirmation. A shared message can influence incident decisions, so private drafting does not authorize publication.

Check that the Slack audience is permitted to see the included Jira, Notion, and account information. If this cannot be established, block posting and explain the access issue.

Customer messages, other destinations, delivery promises, and automatic paging remain blocked. A P0 classification produces a visible “Human attention required” flag.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Run automated structure and evidence checks before displaying the brief:

Include Summary, Key themes, Risks and urgency, and Open questions.

Keep the brief under 250 words.

Confirm that every factual claim cites a source in the captured evidence.

Check that cited evidence supports the claim, including its account, incident, timing, and certainty.

Identify an existing P0 classification accurately; do not invent or change it.

Label interpretations and preserve material disagreements.

Keep requested deadlines and sales statements distinct from confirmed commitments.

Do not infer a shared cause from similar symptoms or a broader trend from one customer report.

The acceptance bar is complete citation coverage and no detected unsupported factual claims, invented commitments, or unauthorized actions.

Automated checks do not guarantee correctness. A human reviews the final draft before any Slack post. After human edits, rerun the checks on the exact version proposed for posting.

**Failure behaviour**

Allow at most one corrective revision within the original turn, time, and cost limits.

Remove unsupported claims and explicitly identify the resulting evidence gaps. Never silently discard the entire response.

If essential evidence is missing or support remains uncertain, display a clearly labelled “Partial — human review required” result. Separate verified findings from unresolved questions.

Block posting while critical verification failures remain unresolved. The user must obtain supporting evidence or remove the affected content, after which Juno reruns the checks.

If the source identity or account cannot be established, show an explanatory failure notice rather than an incident summary. Keep the original source references available for human investigation.
